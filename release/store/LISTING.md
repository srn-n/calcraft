## Main store listing

**App name** (30 max)

**Short description** (80 max)
A calculator that feels great to use: 24 themes, smart tools and maths games.

**Full description** (4,000 max)

A calculator is a simple tool. Bouncy Calculator makes it a joy to use.

Every key bounces. The answer appears while you type. Swipe the display to delete, pull it down to clear, and pull it up to see everything you've worked out today. Pick from 24 themes, each modelled on a real object: a 1987 Braun pocket calculator, a Swiss railway clock, a green-screen terminal, an Apollo flight computer, a typewriter and more.

**A calculator that understands you**
• See the answer as you type, before you press =
• Tap anywhere in your sum to fix a single digit
• One bracket key that knows whether to open or close
• Percent that works like a desk calculator: 200 + 10% = 220
• Write 2π or 3(4) and it just works
• Scientific functions, degrees and radians, memory keys
• A paper-tape history you can search and reuse

**Tools**
• Tip & Split: tip slider, number of people, round up
• Unit converter: length, mass, temperature, area, volume, speed, data and time
• Percent helper: X% of Y, % change and discounts

**Maths games, played on the calculator's own keypad**
• Sprint: 60 seconds of mental maths, with combos
• Make 24: four numbers, each used once, to make 24
• Broken Keys: some keys are cracked. Reach the target anyway
• Estimate: no time to calculate, so guess close

**Private by design**
Bouncy Calculator has no internet access, no ads, no accounts and no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

Made in Sydney by Simply Made Studio.

## Release steps

1. Play Console → Create app → name *Bouncy Calculator*, App, Free.
2. Complete the **App content** answers above and the **Main store listing**.
3. Testing → **Internal testing** → Create release → upload `BouncyCalculator-5.1.0.aab`.
   - When asked about **Play App Signing**, accept *"Let Google manage and protect your app signing key"*. Your `.signing/upload.jks` becomes the upload key.
4. Add yourself as a tester, install from the opt-in link, and check it on your phone.
5. New personal developer accounts must run a **closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 days** before production access. Plan for this.
6. Promote to Production.
