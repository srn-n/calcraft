## What changed in 1.0.9

### 1. Closed scientific panel peeked out above the MC row (1.0.8 regression)
- **Reported** with a phone screenshot: "there is overlap now between the top row & screen". With
  scientific mode **closed**, a strip of key tops showed between the display and the MC / MR / M+ /
  M− / fx row.
- **Cause:**
  - 1.0.8 gave the scientific panel's clipping box extra room (`padding: 3px 8px 10px`, cancelled by
    negative margins) so the open panel's bottom row could show its 3D edge.
  - When the panel is collapsed (`grid-template-rows: 0fr`), that padding still makes a 13 px box,
    and the top of the first hidden row (2nd, DEG, sin, cos, tan) painted through it.
  - The 1.0.8 checks compared key **positions** with the panel closed, which were identical, but not
    **pixels**, so they missed it.
- **Fix** (`src/styles/base.css`):
  - The panel's contents are `visibility: hidden` when it's fully closed.
  - `transition: visibility 0s linear 380ms` keeps them visible until the close animation ends.
  - `.app.is-sci` makes them visible at once (`transition-delay: 0s`) when it opens.
  - Bonus: the hidden keys can no longer be reached with Tab while closed.
- **Checked:**
  - **Closed panel:** the strip between the display and the MC row is **pixel-identical to 1.0.7**
    (max channel difference 0) in Braun, TE, Cyber and Mochi at 411×835 (2×). It's also identical
    after opening and closing the panel again. 1.0.8 differed by 24–40 in every theme.
  - **Open panel:** the bottom row still shows its full rounded 3D edge, so the 1.0.8 fix holds.
