## What changed in 1.0.7

### 1. Scientific mode on phones: smaller keys, not a squashed display (Notion task, "Up next")
- **Asked:** *"The scientific expand overlaps some of the line at bottom of the screen slightly.
  When scientific option is pressed adjust the button size also instead of making the screen
  shrunk."*
- **Measured before**, in portrait with a 24 px bottom inset, which is typical of gesture navigation:

  | Phone | Display (normal → scientific) | Number keys (normal → scientific) |
  |---|---|---|
  | 360×740 | 125 → **84** px | 72 → 67 px |
  | 393×851 | 217 → **85** px | 72 → 72 px |
  | 412×915 | 277 → **137** px | 72 → 72 px |

  Opening the panel took almost all of its 3 extra rows out of the display while the keys barely
  changed.
- **Cause of the bottom overlap:**
  - Key height on phones comes from a formula, `--key-h`, that assumes the header's height.
  - It has a hard minimum (42 px), and the keypad itself could not shrink.
  - When the real header is taller than assumed (Android font size, status bar, a theme's header),
    the keypad was pushed past the bottom edge, over the gesture/navigation line.
- **Built** (`src/styles/base.css`, phones in portrait, scientific mode only):
  - **Display share:** the display now keeps `clamp(96px, 18dvh, 210px)`, about the same as in
    normal mode on most phones. On short phones (680 px tall or less) it keeps 92 px.
  - **Keys pay for the rows:** the number keys shrink to make room for the extra rows.
  - **Shrinkable keypad:** keypad rows are `minmax(30px, var(--key-h))` and `.pad-wrap` may shrink.
    In the compact-header grid layout, the keypad row is `minmax(0, auto)` and the display row
    `minmax(var(--disp-min), 1fr)`. Whatever the header does, the keys give way first, and they never
    overflow the bottom or slide under the display.
  - **Animation:** the panel's open/close animation is unchanged. The keys now shrink smoothly as it
    opens.
- **Measured after** (same phones, 24 px inset):

  | Phone | Display in scientific mode | Number keys in scientific mode |
  |---|---|---|
  | 360×740 | 133 px (was 84) | 57 px |
  | 393×851 | 153 px (was 85) | 58 px |
  | 412×915 | 165 px (was 137) | 66 px |
  | 360×640 | 92 px (was 84) | 51 px |

- **Stress test:** the header was made 30 and 60 px taller than normal to imitate large Android font
  sizes, on 360×640, 360×740, 393×851 and 412×915.
  - The keys shrink (for example 53 → 44 → 35 px on 393×851).
  - Nothing goes past the bottom edge, and the display never overlaps the keys.
  - Before the fix, the keys either overflowed the bottom or ran under the display.
- **Unchanged:** normal mode, desktop, landscape, Tools and Games. The rules only apply to scientific
  mode on portrait phones narrower than 560 px.
