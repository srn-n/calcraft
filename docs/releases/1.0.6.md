## What changed in 1.0.6

### 1. Units conversion cards no longer overlap (visual audit, 2026-11-13, P2)
- **Reported:** in Tools → Units, long converted values ran past their cards into the neighbouring
  card, with a sideways scrollbar. It affected Length, Mass, Area, Volume, Speed, Data and Time.
- **Reproduced:** at 1422×798 (desktop), 360×740 (small phone) and Pixel 7.
  - It was worse on phones, where Temperature was hit too with large inputs.
  - It happened with 10, 123456789 and 0.000123.
- **Cause:** three things combined.
  - The cards were buttons with an inline `all: unset` and no width limit.
  - Grid items default to `min-width: auto`, so one long unbreakable number like `393,700.787401575`
    or `0.00000277777778` widened its column past the card and the panel.
  - Values were shown with up to 12 significant digits.
- **Fix** (`src/ui/tools.js`, `src/styles/base.css`):
  - **Compact card numbers** (`cardNumber`):
    - Normal values show up to 9 significant digits, or 7 below 1.
    - Very small or very large values switch to short scientific notation, for example
      `2.77778E−6` and `4.86047E12`.
    - The To field and each card's tooltip keep full precision, so no precision is lost.
  - **Measured fit** (`fitCards`):
    - Each value shrinks one pixel at a time until it fits on one line, down to 11 px.
    - It refits when the theme font loads or the cards change size.
    - Only below 11 px does it wrap. It never overflows.
  - **CSS:** the inline style became a real class, `.stat.conv`, with `min-width: 0`, a
    `minmax(0, 1fr)` inner column and `overflow: hidden`. `.stat-grid` items get `min-width: 0`, and
    the grid can't exceed its container.
  - **Keyboard:** the cards gained a visible focus ring, which `all: unset` had removed.

### 2. Tool tabs fit on 360 px phones
- **Found while checking 1:** the Tip & Split / Units / Percent / Bills row was 6 px too wide on
  360 px screens, so it scrolled sideways and clipped "Bills".
- **Fix:** below 390 px the gap and padding are slightly tighter. All four tabs now fit.

## Tests and checks
- **145 tests pass.** That's 2 new ones:
  - exact `cardNumber` output for normal, tiny, huge, negative, zero and NaN values;
  - every unit pair in every category stays at 15 characters or fewer for six awkward inputs.
- `npm run build` and `npm run build:android` pass, including the CSP check.
- **Automated layout sweep:**
  - **Coverage:** 3 viewports (1422×798, 360×740, Pixel 7) × 6 themes (Braun, Mochi, Cyber,
    Glass, Nova, Typewriter) × all 8 categories × 4 inputs (10, 123456789, 0.000123, −98765.4321),
    for **3,240 cards**.
  - **Before the fix:** the overlap was confirmed in every category except Temperature on desktop,
    and in all of them on phones.
  - **After the fix:** no card overflows, no value wraps, and Tools has no sideways overflow.
- Before and after screenshots were compared at desktop and 360 px.
