## What changed in 1.0.4

### 1. DEG/RAD and Paste buttons always visible
- **Bug:** a container query hid the whole chip row whenever the display was under about 96 to 150 px
  tall.
  - In **scientific mode**, that hid both buttons in all themes on typical phones.
  - **Voyager** has extra key-row spacing, so it hid them even in normal mode on smaller phones.
- **Fix:** short displays now show a compact chip row instead of hiding it (`src/styles/base.css`).
  - Checked on 360×740, 393×780 and 412×839 phone sizes, normal and scientific mode: always visible.
- Chip colours are mixed toward the display's text colour, so they're clearer on very dark displays
  (TE, Dot Matrix, Darkroom, Neon Ronin, Nova, Soft).

### 2. "Exclusive" badge in the card's bottom-right corner
- The badge is pinned to the bottom-right corner of each Exclusive card, so every theme name lines up
  the same whatever its length.

### 3. Neon Tokyo replaced by Night Weaver (Exclusive)
- **Requested:** a Marvel Spider-Man theme. **Not built:** Spider-Man is a protected Marvel character,
  and Play rejects unlicensed characters.
- **Built instead:** an original masked wall-climbing hero, **Weaver** (`weaver`), in its own theme.
  - Look: midnight blue-black, teal webbing in the corner, a cowl with a teal visor band, amber eyes
    and a hex emblem.
  - No face-web pattern and no red-and-blue, so it doesn't resemble any existing character.
  - The name avoids "Web Crawler", because "Wall-Crawler" is a Spider-Man nickname.
- Keys: the character on `=`, webbing motifs on the operator keys, and a dangling spider on AC.
- Its own sound: a quick swish, with a deep swoop on `=`.
- Saved `neon` and `lcd` choices now map to `weaver` (`RENAMED` in `src/themes.js`).
- There are now **5 Exclusive and 23 Classic themes (28 total)**, ordered Nova, Kage, Weaver, Starla,
  Mochi.
- `LISTING.md` and `05-themes.png` were updated.

### 4. Flash when reopening from recent apps
- **App-side cause (fixed):** on Android 14 and older, leaving with Back faded the app out completely.
  Android took the recent-apps snapshot of that blank screen, and reopening then faded back in from
  blank.
  - Now the leave animation is a short settle (scale 0.975, 70% opacity), and the app snaps back to
    normal instantly once it's in the background (`returnIn(true)`, `goHome()` in `MainActivity`). The
    snapshot and the live app match.
  - Verified in the browser: after leaving, the content opacity returns to 1 with no animation on
    return.
- **Possibly phone-side:**
  - If Android has closed the app in the background to save memory, reopening is a fresh start, and
    the system splash shows briefly. That's expected.
  - On Android 15+ the system handles Back itself, so any remaining flash there comes from the phone's
    own transition.

### 5. Typing in forms
- `src/ui/typing.js` (new):
  - Note boxes grow taller as you type (capped at 45% of the screen, then scroll inside).
  - Single-line fields shrink their text to fit long numbers (down to 13 px).
  - The focused field is kept centred above the on-screen keyboard.
- Verified: an 8-line note grew from 103 to 184 px with no hidden lines, and an 18-digit number fits.
  With a keyboard-sized viewport, the last Percent field stayed visible.
