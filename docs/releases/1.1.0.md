## What changed in 1.1.0

The version goes to **1.1.0**, not 1.0.10, because this release adds a feature (Share app).

### 1. The fx closing animation no longer snaps at the end (Notion task, "Up next")
- **Asked:** "The 'fx' closing animation is slightly odd. Especially at the very end, the button edges
  just quickly snap and merge. Find out what's causing this."
- **How it was found:** a frame-by-frame recording of the close (a CDP screencast at about 60 fps,
  TE theme, 411×835).
- **Cause** (my own, from 1.0.8 and 1.0.9):
  - 1.0.8 gave the panel's clipping box shadow room (`padding: 3px 8px 10px`), so the box can't shrink
    below 13 px.
  - During the close, from about 150 ms to 380 ms, the rest of the layout had finished moving, but a
    13 px strip of the first row's key tops (2nd, DEG, sin, cos, tan) stayed stuck above the MC row.
  - At 380 ms, 1.0.9's `visibility: hidden` removed that strip in a single frame. That was the snap.
- **Fix** (`src/styles/base.css`, `.sci > div`):
  - Closing: the keys fade out in the first 150 ms (`opacity 150ms ease-out`), before the box reaches
    its minimum size. `visibility` still switches off at 380 ms, once they're invisible.
  - Opening: the keys fade in as the panel grows (`opacity 220ms ease-out 60ms`).
- **Checked:**
  - **Close:** frames at 35, 93, 159, 208, 259, 306, 340, 373, 405 and 454 ms. From 159 ms on there's
    no strip, nothing left above the MC row, and nothing disappears in a single frame.
  - **Open:** frames at 29–455 ms. The keys fade in smoothly as the rows grow, with no strip.
  - **Closed panel:** still hidden and not focusable (the 1.0.9 fix holds).
  - **Reduced motion:** unchanged, because the global reduce-motion rules shorten transitions.

### 2. ☰ → Share app (Notion task, "Up next")
- **Asked:** "In the three-bar menu also add a Share the app option just beneath Share feedback. Use
  the app's link."
- **Built:** a new row under *Send feedback*, "Enjoying Bouncy? / Share app".
  - **Android app:** the system share sheet (`Intent.ACTION_SEND` chooser) via a new bridge method,
    `BouncyNative.shareApp()`.
    - It **takes no arguments**. The text and link are fixed in `strings.xml`, so nothing on the page
      can make the app share anything else.
    - It's kept by the existing ProGuard rule for `@JavascriptInterface`.
  - **Phone browsers (web):** the Web Share API (`navigator.share`) with title, text and link. If the
    user cancels the sheet, nothing else happens.
  - **Everywhere else (most desktops):** it copies the Play link, and the existing copy helper shows
    "Copied <link>".
  - **Shared text:** "Bouncy Calculator: a calculator that's actually fun. Bouncy keys, 28 themes,
    handy tools and maths games. Free on Google Play:
    https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.simplymadestudio.bouncycalculator"
  - **Privacy line** in the menu now reads "Nothing leaves your device unless you send feedback or
    share the app."
- **No new Android permissions:** sharing uses the system sheet, and VIBRATE is still the only
  permission.
