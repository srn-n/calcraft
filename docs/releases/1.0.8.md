## What changed in 1.0.8

### 1. Bottom scientific row no longer cut off
- **Reported (after 1.0.7):** "The bottom scientific row still overlaps the border beneath them."
- **Cause:**
  - The scientific panel slides open by animating its grid rows (`0fr → 1fr`). Its inner box
    (`.sci > div`) has `overflow: hidden`, so it clips while closed and during the slide.
  - That clipping also cut everything outside the keys' boxes: the 3D bottom edge (`0 2px 0` shadow)
    and the soft drop shadow of the last row (π e n! 1/x EE), plus the side shadows of the outer
    columns.
  - The result was a flat, hard line and a faint shaded rectangle between the last scientific row and
    the MC/MR row. Box measurements looked fine; only the pixels showed it.
- **Fix** (`src/styles/base.css`):
  - The clipping box gets padding (`3px 8px 10px`) that is cancelled by the same negative margin.
  - The shadows now render inside the clip, and nothing in the layout moves. The panel heights, the
    gap to the MC row and every key position are identical to before. Checked with the panel closed,
    mid-slide and open.
- This applies on every screen size and in every theme.
