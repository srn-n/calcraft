## What changed in 1.1.1

### 1. A seamless opening
- **Asked:** "Improve app opening then the animation. Make it more seamless."
- **How it was found:**
  - A frame-by-frame recording (CDP screencast, about 60 fps) of the whole opening at 412×915.
  - Recorded for the first launch (the full intro) and for repeat launches on Android (the quick
    studio card), in Braun.
  - Then checked in Dot (dark), Liquid Glass, Voyager (gradient body) and Mochi, on a tablet (820 px)
    and on a desktop window.
- **Three seams it showed:**
  1. **A blink on the first frame.**
     - The splash first painted in the backdrop colour (with Braun's dot grain).
     - About 80 ms later, script repainted it in the calculator body's colour.
     - On Android it blinked twice, because the window behind the page is already the body colour.
  2. **An empty dip on the way out.**
     - The whole splash faded out first, and the app only started appearing after that.
     - So for about 150 ms the screen was a washed-out blank.
  3. **Slow repeat launches on Android.**
     - The studio card ran about 1.5 s, holding still for about 0.8 s of it, before the same dip.
     - Usable at about 2.3 s, on top of the system splash.
- **Built:**
  - **The right colour from the first frame.**
    - The splash's body layer comes from CSS (`.splash::after`, `background: var(--device)`) wherever
      the calculator fills the screen.
    - On wider screens, where the app is a card, it keeps the page backdrop, which is what's behind it.
    - The script repaint (`matchScreen`) is gone.
  - **The hand-off (full intro).** The splash's mini calculator grows into the real calculator (a
    shared-element transition, `morphIntoApp` in `splash.js`):
    - 0–160 ms: the splash text and the mini keys fade out.
    - 0–350 ms: the body stretches to the calculator's exact shape, with its corners counter-scaled so
      they stay round.
    - 160–580 ms: the real keys settle in through it while it dissolves.
    - It's transform and opacity only, so it runs on the GPU.
    - On a card layout (560 px and up), the card itself arrives inside the grown body, and the
      desktop side panel follows.
  - **The crossfade (repeat launches, or skipping before the calculator appears).**
    - The backdrop drops away at once. It matches what's behind it, so nothing changes on screen.
    - The app arrives 90 ms after the card starts leaving, and the card leaves slightly faster, so
      there's no gap and no double exposure.
  - **Repeat launches** show the studio card for about 1 s: everything lands by about 700 ms, holds,
    then hands over. Launch to usable is now about 1.35 s.
  - **Android 12+ system splash:** the light background is now the default calculator body colour
    (`icon_bg`, #EFEBE4) instead of the backdrop beige. That's the colour the window hands over to.
  - **Liquid Glass:** its frosted blur couldn't stretch cleanly (it smeared over the keys).
    - The blur is off for the moment the body grows.
    - Being see-through, the body is gone by the time it lands.
- **Not changed:**
  - The intro's content and length on first launch (about 5 s, skippable after the studio name).
  - The reduced-motion version (plain fades, no growing).
  - The "never fade the app fully out when leaving" rule for Android's recent-apps snapshot.

## Tests and checks
- **161 tests pass.** That's 6 new ones in `tests/splash.test.js`:
  - the first-frame body layer, and no script repaint
  - the backdrop drops instead of fading over the app
  - the morph and its counter-scaled corners
  - the app starts arriving before the morph finishes
  - no morph under reduced motion, and the ~1 s quick launch
  - the quick-launch footer doesn't reappear
  - the Android splash colour
- `npm run build` and `npm run build:android` pass, including the CSP check. The morph uses the Web
  Animations API, with no inline styles.
- **Frame recordings, before and after** (Braun, 412×915):
  - The first frame is the body colour, with no second change.
  - The hand-off has no blank frames, and the keys show within a frame of the body landing.
  - The quick launch is usable at about 1.35 s (was 2.3 s), with at most one frame of crossover.
- **Also recorded:** Dot, Liquid Glass, Voyager, Mochi, a tablet (820×1180) and a desktop window
  (1422×798, TE).
- **Edge cases:**
  - Reduced motion.
  - Skipping during the studio card (crossfade) and during the calculator (morph).
  - A key that skips the splash isn't typed into the calculator.
  - No console errors.
- **App smoke test:** all checks pass at 360, 412 and desktop; all 28 themes switch.
- **Not checked here:** a real phone. The Android-only parts are the system splash colour and the
  quick launch inside the WebView. CI builds them on push.
