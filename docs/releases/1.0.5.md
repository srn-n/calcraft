## What changed in 1.0.5

### 1. Smooth, bouncy theme switching
- **Measured before** (Pixel 7 profile, CPU slowed 6× to match a mid-range phone): every switch
  froze for **300 to 600 ms**, with 4 to 10 dropped frames. There were three causes:
  - A full-page View Transition, which snapshots and cross-fades the whole screen including the open
    sheet.
  - About 40 keys, tabs and chips each running a colour transition at the same time.
  - The restyle itself, as every colour and font on the page changes.
- **Now** (`applyTheme` in `src/ui/themes-ui.js`):
  1. A burst of the new theme's colour grows from the card you tapped. In the open picker it sits
     behind the cards, so the cards stay readable.
  2. While the burst covers the screen, the theme swaps in one frame with all CSS transitions paused.
     The restyle happens out of sight.
  3. The burst fades away, the picked card pops (squash, overshoot, settle) and the grid gives a small
     bounce.
  4. With the sheet closed (Shuffle from the palette, "Surprise me"), the keys ripple-bounce outward
     from the tap.
- Theme fonts are preloaded when the picker opens, so a switch never waits on a font.
- **Measured after:** a single restyle frame per switch, hidden under the burst. All other frames are
  smooth.
- Rapid tapping always lands on the last theme you picked. Taps during the burst retarget it; taps
  while it fades switch instantly. Tested with 0, 60, 200, 380 and 500 ms between taps.
- Reduced motion: an instant swap with no burst.

### 2. Exclusive sound sets (`src/fx/signature.js`, new)
Each Exclusive character now has its own instrument. The number keys play notes from that
character's scale, so typing a sum plays a little tune.

| Character | Number keys | = | Theme pick jingle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nova (astro) | Space-radio boops with an echo | Launch arpeggio and a sparkle | Radio crackle, beep-boop, lift-off |
| Kage (ronin) | Koto plucks, Japanese *in* scale | Taiko hit and a sword ring | Taiko, blade draw, koto phrase |
| Weaver | Minor-key ticks | **Thwip**, then a landing thud | Double thwip, swoop, minor run |
| Starla (pinkpop) | Glockenspiel bells | Sparkle run and a "ta-da" chord | Pop, sparkle run, chord |
| Mochi (kitten) | Soft marimba | A little "mew" | Purr, mew, marimba |

- Each character also has its own operator, AC and error sounds.
- All sounds are synthesised, with no audio files.
- The sounds play through a limiter, so chords never distort at the High setting.
- Mashing keys skips a sound rather than piling up voices.
- They follow the existing Sound level and the phone's silent mode.
- Classic themes are unchanged.

### 3. Living characters (`src/ui/buddy.js`, new)
The character on `=` is now its own layer (`::before`), so it can move independently of the key.
It also mirrors in the header on taller screens.

| | Nova | Kage | Weaver | Starla | Mochi |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **=** | Rocket hop, stars burst and a shooting star crosses the display | Lunge, a blade slash streaks across the display, sparks | Swings, a web line shoots to the display and a web pops | Spins, hearts and sparkles float up | Squash-and-stretch hop, paw prints walk across the display and a heart |
| **AC** | Spins | Ninja vanish | Zips up and drops back | Wiggle | Ducks down and peeks back |
| **Idle (12 s)** | Floats with twinkles | Meditates, "z"s | Hangs upside down from a thread | Dances with music notes | Falls asleep, "z"s |

- **Typing:** a nod on each digit and a lean on operators, at most one per beat.
- **Error:** shakes its head, and a "?" pops up.
- **Game wins** trigger the celebration too.
- **Theme pick:** the character pops in with its effect. If you picked from the sheet, it waits until
  you close the sheet so you actually see it.
- **Cost:** effects are a few short-lived elements in one fixed layer. Only transform and opacity
  animate, and nothing runs on Classic themes, with reduced motion, or in the background.
- **Weaver note:** Weaver stays original (teal, amber, a cowl and a visor). The web effects are
  generic spider imagery, not any existing character's look.
